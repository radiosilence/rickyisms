Rickyisms.

Ricky's malapropisms and eggcorns: phrases that sound about right and mean something else entirely. Collected from the series, the specials, the movies and the podcast.

Notes from the wiki

  • Make like a tree and fuck off. Probably intentional: “fuck off” is Ricky's standard way of getting people to leave him alone.
  • Bumblecocksucker is almost certainly intentional.
  • Fire retarded is a real word per Merriam-Webster, meaning “protected with a fire-retardant material”. It still counts, since it isn't what he meant.
  • Zesty Mordant comes from Canadian bilingual packaging. The Doritos bag printed the English “Zesty” and the French “Mordant” (“biting”) large, with the rest small, so “Zesty Cheese” and “Fromage Mordant” collapsed into one name.
  • The longest word Ricky is known to say correctly is reportedly re-allocated, in season four, episode one.
